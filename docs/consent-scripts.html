Toll-Free Verification scripts

Example Verbal Consent Script

“Hi, I’d like to confirm that you agree to receive text messages from me through my toll-free number. These texts will only be for personal communication, not marketing. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Do you agree?”

Example Written Consent Script

“By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from me through my toll-free number. These messages are for personal communication only and are not promotional. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.”
