Use Case Description 
Personal two-way SMS relay between two known contacts. The toll-free number is used solely to forward SMS messages between the account owner and a single external contact for personal, non-promotional communication. Message and data rates may apply.
Verbal Consent Script 
"Hi, this is [Your Name]. I want to confirm you agree to receive text messages from my toll-free number [Your Toll-Free Number]. These messages will be for personal communication only (not marketing). Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Do you agree?"
Written Consent Example 
By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from [Your Name] through the toll-free number [Your Toll-Free Number]. These messages are for personal communication only and are not promotional. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
Sample Messages (use in "Message Samples" field)
Inbound (from external contact to toll-free): 
Hey — are we still meeting tonight at 7? — Alice
Forwarded (from toll-free to you): 
FWD from +1XXXXXXXXXX: Hey — are we still meeting tonight at 7?
Opt-out / STOP Language 
Reply STOP to opt out of future messages. After replying STOP you will no longer receive messages from this number.
Additional Notes for Submission
